Q…P: fingers on the bottom strip (several = sequence or poly) while the mouse plays scatter. Pressure: drag up/down, wheel over a finger, or ↑↓. Shift+click pins a finger, Esc clears pins. C: sequence/poly. Space: REC, H: HOLD (with REC: endless 8 s loop), M: source, N: the name (Shift+N: softly). 1…5: effect at full while held. Everything springs back on release, HOLD keeps it.
